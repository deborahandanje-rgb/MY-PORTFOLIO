JusticeLink Kenya

Know Your Rights. Find Support. Take Action.

The Challenge

People may know that SGBV is wrong, but they may not know their rights, what responsible action looks like, or where to find trustworthy support. Silence, stigma, and limited access to resources often prevent survivors from speaking out.

Who It Serves

  • Survivors of sexual violence
  • Young people and communities
  • Advocates and support organisations
  • Human rights defenders
  • Community leaders and educators

The Solution

The platform will translate complex legal and rights information into simple, accessible educational content, while connecting users with verified organisations, services, and resources.

Rather than being only a reporting website, JusticeLink Kenya will focus on: Know → Connect → Act

KNOW Understand your rights and available justice pathways.

CONNECT Find relevant and verified support organisations and resources.

ACT Take informed, safe, and responsible action while contributing to prevention and community awareness.

Core Pillars of JusticeLink Kenya

Legal & Rights Education

JusticeLink Kenya translates complex legal and human-rights information into clear, easy-to-understand resources, helping people understand their rights, available justice pathways, and protections related to SGBV.

Survivor Support & Referral

The platform helps survivors and affected individuals identify verified organisations and appropriate support resources, reducing the difficulty of navigating available services.

Prevention & Community Education

Equips young people, families, educators and communities with knowledge and practical resources to understand, prevent and respond appropriately to SGBV.

Community Voice & Advocacy

Provides opportunities for communities, young people, advocates and human-rights defenders to engage in conversations around justice, equality, safety and accountability.

Safety, Accountability & Justice

EThe platform encourages informed, survivor-centred and responsible responses to SGBV while promoting accountability and challenging stigma, victim-blaming and harmful social norms.

Project Status

JusticeLink Kenya is currently in the design and development phase as part of my web development and social innovation journey. The platform continues to evolve with the goal of creating safer and more informed communities.

The Inspiration

Inspired by Bungoma County, Kenya (County No. 39), 30Nine Visuals is a creative venture that seeks to celebrate African stories, artistic expression, and contemporary design through digital and visual media.
